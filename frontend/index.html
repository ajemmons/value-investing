Backtesting & weight tuning

Scoring weights for each risk level are tuned by a bias-aware backtesting engine that tests rolling time windows, compares to the S&P 500, penalizes sector concentration and over-reliance on a few extreme winners, and compares rebalancing methods. Read the limitations carefully — the bundled run uses illustrative synthetic data.
